TechNova Remote Working Policy — FICTIONAL TEST DOCUMENT
Version 1.0. Invented data for the CompanyMind learning project.

Remote working allowance
Employees may request up to 2 remote working days per week.
The reporting manager must approve the arrangement in writing.

Request process
Submit the requested dates through the employee portal at least 1 working day
before the first requested remote working day.

Equipment and security
Use company-approved equipment and the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
