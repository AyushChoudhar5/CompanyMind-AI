TechNova Remote Working Policy
FICTIONAL LEARNING POLICY. This document expands the fictional general employment policy; it does not override individual employment terms.

Remote work approval
Employees request remote working from their reporting manager and HR. Remote work is not automatically approved. Agree the work location and schedule before starting the arrangement.

Standard arrangement
An approved standard arrangement allows up to 2 remote working days per working week, subject to role requirements. The manager and HR must confirm the arrangement in writing.

Remote work during probation
Employees on probation may request the same remote working arrangement. Probation does not automatically prevent remote work, but the reporting manager and HR must approve it in writing.

Availability and security
Employees working remotely must follow their agreed schedule, remain reachable through approved work channels and use approved company devices and systems for company information.

Changes and exceptions
Requests for more than 2 remote working days per week require a specific written exception from the manager and HR. Confirm a changed arrangement before relying on it.

Employment terms
The standard probation period remains 6 months. If an individual offer letter differs from general employment policy, identify the difference and contact hr@example.invalid; do not assume which term overrides another.
