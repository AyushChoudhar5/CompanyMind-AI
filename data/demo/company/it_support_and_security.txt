TechNova IT Support and Security
FICTIONAL LEARNING POLICY. All contact addresses are non-operational examples.

VPN troubleshooting
If the company VPN will not connect, check the internet connection, restart the VPN client and reauthenticate with the company account. If it still fails, contact it@example.invalid.

Password and account help
Contact it@example.invalid if a company account is locked. Use the approved password-reset process. Never send passwords, recovery codes or authentication codes to CompanyMind or another person.

Lost device or suspicious access
Report a lost laptop, unexpected login or suspected compromised account to it@example.invalid immediately. Include the device or account affected and when the incident was noticed. Never include a password or code.

Software access requests
Ask the reporting manager to approve the business need for a new tool. IT checks the approved software list and provisions access after approval. CompanyMind explains the process but cannot grant access.

Security training
Complete security awareness training in the first working week. Use approved systems for employee documents and do not copy confidential company data into unapproved public tools.

Support coverage
Routine IT support coverage in this fictional demo is Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time, excluding company holidays. No response-time guarantee or 24-hour incident service is defined.
