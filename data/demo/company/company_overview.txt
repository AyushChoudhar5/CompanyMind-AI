TechNova Company Overview
FICTIONAL LEARNING DATA. TechNova, its staff, products and prices in this library are invented for CompanyMind demonstrations.

Business
TechNova builds workplace software that helps small and mid-sized businesses manage employee support requests, internal approvals and company knowledge.

History and offices
TechNova was founded in 2021. Its headquarters is in Bengaluru, India, and it has a second office in Pune, India. These are fictional locations for this demo.

Mission
TechNova's mission is to make everyday work easier by connecting employees with clear information and dependable internal tools.

Customers
The fictional target customers are growing businesses with 50 to 1000 employees. TechNova serves teams such as HR, IT, operations and customer support.

Leadership
Aarav Mehta is the fictional founder and CEO. Kavya Rao is the fictional CTO. Meera Shah is the fictional Head of People. These names are invented sample data.

Values
TechNova values clear communication, customer usefulness, responsible data handling and practical learning. Employees should ask the relevant team when a policy is unclear.
