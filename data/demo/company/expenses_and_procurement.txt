TechNova Expenses and Procurement
FICTIONAL LEARNING POLICY. Amounts and deadlines below are invented company rules for this demo.

Expense submission
Submit a business expense claim through the employee portal within 30 calendar days of the expense. Attach the itemized receipt and describe the business purpose. The reporting manager reviews the claim.

Reimbursement processing
Finance processes approved claims in the next monthly reimbursement cycle. This policy does not provide a payment date or live claim status. Contact finance@example.invalid for the status of a specific claim.

Travel booking
Obtain the reporting manager's written approval before booking work travel. Keep the approval and receipts with the expense claim. This document defines no hotel, meal or airfare reimbursement limits.

Equipment purchases
Request equipment through IT before purchasing it yourself. IT checks compatibility and the available inventory. Manager approval is required before a purchase request is sent to Finance.

Procurement threshold
A single purchase of INR 25000 or more requires Finance approval in addition to manager approval. Purchases below INR 25000 still require manager approval. Do not split purchases to avoid approval.

Supplier payments
Only Finance authorizes supplier payments after the required approvals and invoice checks. CompanyMind cannot submit a claim, approve a purchase or transfer money.
