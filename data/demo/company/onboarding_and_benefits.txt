TechNova Onboarding and Benefits Guide
FICTIONAL LEARNING POLICY. These sample benefits are not statements about a real employer.

Before joining
HR shares the joining checklist and asks the new employee to provide required documents through approved systems. HR creates the employee profile; IT prepares a company laptop and company email.

First working week
Meet the reporting manager, agree work expectations and complete security awareness training in the first working week. Ask HR about missing employment documents and IT about device or account issues.

Learning budget
Confirmed full-time employees may request a learning budget of up to INR 10000 per financial year for approved role-related courses. Obtain written manager approval before purchasing a course.

Learning budget during probation
The standard learning budget becomes available after written confirmation of probation completion. HR can clarify whether an individually agreed exception applies. The standard probation period is 6 months.

Leave entitlement
The fictional leave policy provides full-time employees 12 casual leave days, 10 sick leave days and 18 annual leave days per calendar year. Only up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry forward.

Benefits questions
Contact hr@example.invalid for benefits questions. This library does not define medical insurance coverage, maternity leave terms or employee-specific eligibility beyond the rules explicitly stated here.
