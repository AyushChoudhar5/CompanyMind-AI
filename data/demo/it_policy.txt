TechNova IT and Onboarding Guide
FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Contact addresses are non-operational examples.

Onboarding
HR creates the employee profile. IT issues a company laptop and company email.
Complete security awareness training in the first working week.

VPN troubleshooting
Check your internet connection and restart the VPN client. Reauthenticate using
your company account. If access still fails, contact it@example.invalid.

Account security
Do not share passwords or authentication codes. Report a lost laptop to IT.
Only use approved systems for employee documents.
