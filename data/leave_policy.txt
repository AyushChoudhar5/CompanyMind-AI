TechNova Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-001
Version: 1.0

This is a fictional company policy for a learning project.

Casual Leave
Full-time employees receive 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Employees should request casual leave at least 2 working days in advance.
Unused casual leave does not carry forward to the next year.

Sick Leave
Full-time employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive sick days.

Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry forward to the next year.

Approval
All leave requests must be submitted through the employee portal.
The employee's reporting manager approves or rejects the request.
